Privacy Policy for Focus Habitat

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Focus Habitat is developed and published by Sedyoo. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Focus Habitat.

1. Game Progress

Focus Habitat stores gameplay progress locally on your device. This may include information such as Focus Coins, owned items, biome progression, focus history, streaks, habitat placements, and other gameplay state.

Focus Habitat does not require you to create a separate Sedyoo account.

2. Google Play Games Services

Focus Habitat may use Google Play Games Services to provide:

  • Google Play Games authentication
  • Player profile information
  • Cloud backup and restore through Google Play Games Saved Games

If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games account according to Google's own privacy policies.

Game progress may be stored using Google Play Games Saved Games when you manually back up your progress or when Auto Backup is enabled.

Focus Habitat does not operate its own remote server for storing your Google account credentials.

3. Advertising

Focus Habitat uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are shown only after you choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an optional in-game reward. Your normal Focus session reward does not depend on watching an advertisement.

Google AdMob may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information required to provide and measure advertising.

Depending on your region and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Focus Habitat uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

Users in applicable regions may be given options to consent, decline consent, or manage their privacy choices.

Changing or declining advertising consent does not prevent you from using the core Focus Habitat focus timer and habitat gameplay features.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Save and restore gameplay progress
  • Provide Google Play Games functionality
  • Provide optional rewarded advertisements
  • Prevent duplicate rewards or abuse
  • Maintain application stability and security
  • Diagnose technical problems

6. Data Storage

Gameplay progress may exist in:

  • Local application storage on your device
  • Google Play Games Saved Games, if cloud backup is used

Local safety backups may also be created before a cloud restore operation to help protect existing progress.

Sedyoo does not currently operate a separate proprietary cloud database containing Focus Habitat gameplay accounts.

7. Third-Party Services

Focus Habitat currently uses Google services including:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Games Saved Games
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

Sedyoo does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google when necessary to provide authentication, cloud saves, advertising, consent management, security, or related functionality.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Use the core game without watching rewarded advertisements
  • Choose whether to connect Google Play Games
  • Turn Auto Backup on or off
  • Manually back up or restore cloud progress
  • Manage advertising privacy choices where available
  • Remove local application data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application

Google-hosted information may also be subject to controls available through your Google Account and Google services.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary data collection and protect gameplay data. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Focus Habitat adds, removes, or changes features or third-party services.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
